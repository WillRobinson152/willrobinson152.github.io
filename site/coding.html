Coding

Robinson has earned professional certification for coding and created dozens of automated workflows used by media companies.

Education & work history

Robinson is proficient in Python, SQL and full stack development (MongoDB, Express, React, Node), having earned professional certifications in coding from MIT xPro and data science from Springboard.

As lead editor of data and automation at Forbes Advisor, Robinson created and maintained automated pipelines that kept more than 275 pages and thousands of data visualizations up to date, including by connections to various third-party data sources and programmatic page updates. He wrote Python modules to standardize his team's methodology on automation projects and created logging processes to alert the team of issues related to data sources or page updates.

Earlier in his career at OPIS (now Dow Jones Energy), Robinson created desktop applications for his team that automated and simplified processes for recordkeeping and price assessing, automated the process of writing the first drafts of data-pegged stories (propane inventory levels, commitments of traders reports, etc.) to make sure OPIS was always the first to publish, created an interface for non-technical teammates to use the company's API and created some of the company's first data visualizations.
