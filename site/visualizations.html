Forbes Advisor

As lead editor of data and automation at Forbes Advisor, Robinson was responsible for building and maintaining automated workflows that pulled internal and third-party data, transformed and cleaned it and pushed it to thousands of data visualizations and hundreds of programmatic on-page text updates. Charts needed to provide the latest available data, label their update date and source and keep brand style guidelines.

HousingWire

Robinson was HousingWire's first data journalist and was therefore able to help decide on the data visualization vendor selection process and work with designers to shape the HousingWire's data visualization aesthetic. He built hundreds of visualizations in his time there, both for his own reporting and for the broader newsroom. His visualization-filled reporting won SABEW's Data Journalism award, small division, in 2023.
