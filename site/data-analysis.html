Data Analysis

Robinson holds an M.S. in Data Analytics Engineering and has carried out ambitious data projects.

Education & work history

Robinson graduated from Northeastern University with a Master of Science in Data Analytics Engineering in 2026. He completed the program while working full-time, first at Forbes Advisor and later at Evertune AI, and earned a 3.95 GPA.

Throughout his career in journalism, Robinson has undertaken many data-heavy projects. At the Jacksonville Business Journal, he completed projects on Covid-related layoffs, Paycheck Protection Program loan recipients, the dichotomy between a railroad's filings with regulators and investors and many more. At HousingWire, Robinson served as a bridge between the newsroom and data partners, which required creating and querying various databases and creating data visualizations used by nine other reporters. He also reported extensively on this data, winning a Data Journalism award from SABEW in 2023.

At Forbes Advisor, Robinson connected myriad data sources via API to the company's automated pipelines for content and visualization updates while ensuring data accuracy, timeliness and appropriate context. Most recently at Evertune AI, he mined the company's internal data to find updates and trends related to marketing for AI search (a field known as Generative Engine Optimization, or GEO). This required extensive work in MongoDB and BigQuery, as well as employing data cleaning, wrangling, analysis, visualization and storytelling skills.
