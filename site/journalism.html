Journalism

Robinson has covered numerous business journalism beats over the last decade, and his work has won acclaim from several professional news associations. He has also created editorial marketing content.

Selected work

From January through October 2026, Robinson was Evertune AI's AI Insights Editor, where he mined the company's in-house data, found insights and trends and shared his findings through white papers, blogs, sponsored articles, LinkedIn posts and conferences, including a presentation at brightonSEO in San Diego.

From May 2024 to January 2026, Robinson was the lead editor of data and automation at Forbes Advisor. Rather than a traditional reporting role, this was a highly technical role responsible for creating and maintaining automated workflows that kept more than 275 articles and thousands of data visualizations up-to-date and appropriately modified based on a myriad of third-party data sources.

In July 2023, Robinson became HousingWire's first data journalist, where he helped set the visualization aesthetic and brand voice of the newsroom's data coverage.

Robinson reported on energy commodity markets as Senior Editor, Natural Gas Liquids, at OPIS (now Dow Jones Energy) from April 2021 to May 2023. In this role, he was both a price assessor and market commentator, reporting on price movements and their causes for propane, ethane and the like for hubs throughout North America.

Robinson joined the Jacksonville Business Journal in 2017 and covered logistics, technology and finance. He later was promoted to cover the commercial real estate and economic development beats.
